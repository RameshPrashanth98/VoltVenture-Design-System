## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- **WORKTREE-ISOLATION**: Do not run executor agents with `isolation:"worktree"` — they lose Bash permission intermittently and fail to commit. Use foreground agents only.
- **STAGING-FOUNDATION-STORIES**: Do not `git add stories/` broadly — foundation story files (border/color/elevation/grid/iconography/radius/spacing/typography) have pre-existing modifications and must not be staged.
- **LIB-MUTATION**: Always `git restore lib/voltventure_theme.ts lib/voltventure_tokens.ts` immediately after any build-storybook run.
- **LOWERCASE-EXPORTS**: All story exports must be PascalCase (Default, Interactive, SourceCode). Storybook 10 silently ignores lowercase exports.
